# Privacy

The mods in this repository collect nothing, store nothing outside your own
machine, and send nothing anywhere.

Gas Gauge and Speedometer read only the figures Claude Code already holds
about your session: how much of your plan's usage windows is used, when they
reset, and how full the context window is. They keep those figures in Claude
Code's own session state so a reload does not reset the dial. Nothing leaves
your machine, and there is no server, account, analytics or tracking.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/DJPalefaceSD/rostech-mods/issues
